Helpful Information

Quick answers before you reach out to support.

  • Forgot your password? Use the link on the sign-in page to reset it by email.
  • Didn't get a confirmation email? Check spam, and make sure you typed your address correctly at sign up.
  • Using Google sign-in? Your account is matched by the email on your Google account.
  • Trouble signing in? Double-check for extra spaces in your email field.

Still stuck? Contact support and we'll help you sort it out.
